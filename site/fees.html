FAQ

Questions people often ask

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. Brenda offers a free 15-minute consultation by phone. You can share what you’re looking for, ask questions, and see whether it feels like a good fit. Call (609) 326-3955 or use the contact page.

Do you take my insurance?

Brenda is in network with Aetna, AmeriHealth, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Horizon BCBS NJ, Carelon, Cigna/Evernorth, Optum, Oscar, Oxford, UMR, and UnitedHealthcare. The quickest way to check your plan and estimated cost is Brenda’s Headway page.

What if you’re out of network with my plan?

Out-of-network clients are welcome. The self-pay rate is $175 per session, paid by cash, check, or PayPal.

Do you offer online sessions?

Yes. Brenda sees clients online across New Jersey, as well as in person at her home office in Bordentown.

Who do you work with?

Infants and toddlers (with their parents), children, preteens, teens, adults, and seniors 65 and older, in individual, family, and couples sessions, including premarital concerns. The practice is LGBTQIA+ affirming.

What happens in the first session?

It’s a chance to get to know each other. Brenda will ask about what brings you in and what you’d like to change, and you can ask her anything. There’s no pressure to share more than you’re ready to.

What is your approach?

Brenda practices client-centered therapy: your goals lead, and she draws on techniques such as CBT, play therapy, PCIT, art therapy, and motivational interviewing to fit you. See the services page for plain-language descriptions.

Is therapy confidential?

In general, what you share in therapy stays private. Like every therapist, Brenda follows legal exceptions, for example when there is a safety concern. She’s happy to explain how this works before you begin.

What if I’m in crisis?

Therapy here is not an emergency service. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or text HOME to 741741. In an emergency, call 911.

Questions about cost?

Ask away. It’s a free call.

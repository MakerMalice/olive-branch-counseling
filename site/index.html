Meet Brenda

Hello, I’m Brenda.

I’m a licensed clinical social worker with over ten years in practice. My background in community therapy gave me a broad range of experience with people of every age and walk of life.

In private practice I get to focus fully on what brings each person in. I see clients in my home office, which gives our time together a more personal, less clinical feel. You can expect an open, nonjudgmental space where your goals lead the way.

My approach

Your goals lead. The tools follow.

I practice client-centered therapy, drawing on different techniques to fit each person rather than fitting each person to one method.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

Practical skills for noticing unhelpful thought patterns and changing how you respond to them.

Play Therapy

Children often say with toys and games what they can’t yet put into words. Play gives them a safe way in.

Parent-Child Interaction Therapy

Coaching parents in the moment to strengthen the bond with their child and ease challenging behavior.

Art Therapy

Drawing, painting and making things as another way to explore feelings, for kids and adults alike.

Motivational Interviewing

A collaborative conversation that helps you find your own reasons for change.

About Brenda

Warm, steady support, at your pace.

Brenda Alpert, LCSW. More than ten years of helping children, teens, adults and families.

Hello, I’m Brenda.

I’m a licensed clinical social worker, and I’ve been in practice for over ten years. I earned my MSW at Rutgers University in New Brunswick.

I started out in community therapy, where I worked with people of every age and all kinds of challenges. That gave me a wide range of experience, and I still draw on it every day.

About five years ago I moved into private practice. I love being able to focus fully on what matters to the people I work with, without anything else pulling at our time together.

I see clients in my home office in Bordentown, which keeps things comfortable and personal rather than clinical. I also meet with people online anywhere in New Jersey.

Working together

What it’s like to work with me

Clients can expect an open and nonjudgmental relationship with me. I am here to help you identify what changes you need to live your life in the best way for you. Brenda Alpert, LCSW

That means you can say what’s on your mind without worrying about how it will sound. You don’t need to have it figured out before we begin.

Your goals lead the way. I practice client-centered therapy, which means I draw from different techniques and tailor them to you, instead of asking you to fit one method.

Depending on who I’m working with, that might include Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Play Therapy, Parent-Child Interaction Therapy, Art Therapy or Motivational Interviewing.
